# Growth — workflow contract

**Mission:** Attribution and product-led experiments.

1. Resolve tenant and authoritative evidence. If missing, mark limitation.
2. Receive accepted structured input: current actual event taxonomy, consent status, baseline reports, campaign IDs.
3. Perform only approved local transformation or read-only research; capture provenance and licensing.
4. Deliver: experiment cards, actual/synthetic marked dashboards, funnel/cohort findings and PR proposal.
5. Review factual validity, tenant voice, accessibility, privacy, legal constraints and deterministic reproducibility.
6. Submit to next department; never conflate a draft handoff with public posting.

**Stop rule:** No fabricated conversions, fake causality, production deploy or live market mutations.
