# Relationships — workflow contract

**Mission:** DevRel partnerships and founder opportunities.

1. Resolve tenant and authoritative evidence. If missing, mark limitation.
2. Receive accepted structured input: consent-compatible public first-party information, owner-defined target and value.
3. Perform only approved local transformation or read-only research; capture provenance and licensing.
4. Deliver: qualified relationship briefs, personalized unsent drafts, follow-up suggestions.
5. Review factual validity, tenant voice, accessibility, privacy, legal constraints and deterministic reproducibility.
6. Submit to next department; never conflate a draft handoff with public posting.

**Stop rule:** No unsolicited bulk messages, contact harvesting or impersonation.
