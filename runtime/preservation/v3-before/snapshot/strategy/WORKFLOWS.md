# Strategy — workflow contract

**Mission:** ICP, positioning and growth strategy.

1. Resolve tenant and authoritative evidence. If missing, mark limitation.
2. Receive accepted structured input: verified source cards, market segments, known customer jobs and current product constraints.
3. Perform only approved local transformation or read-only research; capture provenance and licensing.
4. Deliver: audience/problem/proof hypothesis, campaign priority, channel plan, activation definition and measurement owners.
5. Review factual validity, tenant voice, accessibility, privacy, legal constraints and deterministic reproducibility.
6. Submit to next department; never conflate a draft handoff with public posting.

**Stop rule:** No invented TAM, unverified product features or made-up funnel performance.
