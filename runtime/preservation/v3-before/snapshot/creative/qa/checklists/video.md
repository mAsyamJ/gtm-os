# Video QA checklist

- [ ] sourced storyboard, consent and real/synthetic labeling
- [ ] editable timeline/source project included
- [ ] 16:9/9:16 output readability and clipping
- [ ] ffprobe confirms fps, resolution, codec and yuv420p
- [ ] audio peak, clipping, black frames and silence reviewed
- [ ] captions legible and accurately synchronized
- [ ] licenses for footage, music, sound, fonts and voice model
- [ ] private identifiers masked, no fabricated market conditions
