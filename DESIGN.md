# DESIGN.md — System-wide creative contract

## Visual truth

The RetroPick visual identity MUST be taken from current approved artwork and production/staging source. Founder identity is independent; no assumed personal logo. If assets are missing, output obvious neutral placeholder previews and mark `BRAND_ASSETS_PENDING`, not a forged logo or made-up exact hex palette.

## Hierarchy and accessibility

Use deliberate title/subtitle/CTA hierarchy, generous spacing, restrained color usage, legible typography, accessible contrast, consistent grid, device crop safety, and descriptive alt text. Avoid decorative fake charts and illegible trading data. No invented trading volume, yield, returns or real wallet information in imagery.

## Design token sources

`brands/retropick/tokens.json` and `brands/founder/tokens.json` are **provisional sample tokens**, explicitly not verified official brand colors. `creative/design-system/` defines component templates. A verified first-party design audit should update the tokens before production use.

## Image outputs

Maintain editable SVG/source projects, PNG/WebP exports with exact dimensions, templates at 1200×675, 1600×900, 1080×1080 and 1080×1920 as initial candidates (reverify platform specs), plus source and copyright/license metadata. Validate overflow, crop, typography, contrast and content accuracy.

## Video outputs

Each promo requires brief, script, storyboard, shot list, capture source, editable timeline, captions, audio rights, ffprobe manifest, accessible thumbnail and QA. Deliver 16:9 and 9:16 variants with browser-compatible H.264 yuv420p MP4; audio and asset licenses must be separately verified. Never use unapproved likeness/voice clone.

Refer to `creative/DESIGN.md`, `brands/retropick/DESIGN.md`, `brands/founder/DESIGN.md` for scoped styling and veto criteria.
