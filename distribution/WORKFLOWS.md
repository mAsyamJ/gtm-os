# Distribution — workflow contract

**Mission:** Account-safe publishing and creator/community distribution.

1. Resolve tenant and authoritative evidence. If missing, mark limitation.
2. Receive accepted structured input: final approved content/media SHA256, expected tenant/account, platform rules and spend cap.
3. Perform only approved local transformation or read-only research; capture provenance and licensing.
4. Deliver: provider receipt or explicit blocked/manual-ready package; reconciliation log.
5. Review factual validity, tenant voice, accessibility, privacy, legal constraints and deterministic reproducibility.
6. Submit to next department; never conflate a draft handoff with public posting.

**Stop rule:** No posting, outreach, scheduling or ads by default; OAuth identity must match.
