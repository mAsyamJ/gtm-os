# Creative — workflow contract

**Mission:** Image/video creative production.

1. Resolve tenant and authoritative evidence. If missing, mark limitation.
2. Receive accepted structured input: approved narrative + valid brand tokens + fully licensed source assets.
3. Perform only approved local transformation or read-only research; capture provenance and licensing.
4. Deliver: editable graphics, source video timeline, renders, manifest, accessible captions and QA.
5. Review factual validity, tenant voice, accessibility, privacy, legal constraints and deterministic reproducibility.
6. Submit to next department; never conflate a draft handoff with public posting.

**Stop rule:** No misleading UI, fake charts, unlicensed media, or unapproved likeness.
